Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you apply. Still have a question? Come to our general body meeting or reach out.

Do I need clinical experience?

No. We’re looking for reliable, curious students who want to contribute.

What will I do on a shift?

You spend your whole shift with pediatric patients at Children’s Memorial Hermann: talking with them, playing with them, and being a source of support. Training and orientation prepare you before you start.

When would I start?

Committee members start right after decisions on October 25. Volunteers complete about two months of onboarding this fall and begin at CMH next semester.

What does onboarding involve?

Vaccinations, HIPAA clearance, volunteer training, and orientation. We guide you through every step alongside CMH, and you’ll get a clear checklist when you’re accepted.

How much time does it take?

Volunteers give 4 hours per week, in two 2-hour shifts. Committee members give about 1 to 2 hours per week.

Can I volunteer and be on a committee?

Yes, and many members do both. It isn’t required.

How are members selected?

Volunteer spots are determined each semester based on CMH capacity, and each committee is a small team of about 3 to 4 people. Interviews are on October 20 and decisions go out October 25. If you applied for more than one role, your interview covers each one.

What happens at the interview?

A conversation. We’ll ask about working with pediatric patients if you applied to volunteer, and a few questions about each committee you chose.

Do I have to be premed?

No. You do not have to be premed. You just need to be passionate and reliable.

What if I miss the October 15 deadline?

You can apply in the next application cycle for the Fall semester when we open those applications.

Where can I ask questions?

Come to our general body meeting on Monday, October 5 at 6:00 PM in McMurtry PDR, or email ricechampsorg@gmail.com.
