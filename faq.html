Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you apply. Still have a question? Come to our general body meeting or reach out.

Do I need clinical experience?

No. We’re looking for reliable, curious students who want to contribute.

Which volunteer programs are there?

Two this spring: pediatric volunteering at Children’s Memorial Hermann (CMH), and volunteering at TIRR Memorial Hermann, a rehabilitation setting. Both are in the Texas Medical Center.

Can I apply to both CMH and TIRR Memorial Hermann?

Yes. There is one application. You can choose one program, or rank both in order of preference. You can only volunteer with one program, so you’ll receive one offer.

How is my placement decided?

After interviews, we place each student in a single program based on the interview, your ranking, and each hospital’s capacity. If you ranked both, you may be placed in either one.

What will I do on a shift?

At CMH, you spend your whole shift with pediatric patients: talking with them, playing with them, and being a source of support. At TIRR Memorial Hermann, you’ll volunteer with patients in a rehabilitation setting, and we’ll share more details as onboarding begins. Training and orientation prepare you before you start.

When would I start?

Committee members start right after decisions on October 25. Volunteers complete about two months of onboarding this fall and begin at their hospital next semester.

What does onboarding involve?

Vaccinations, HIPAA clearance, volunteer training, and orientation, the same for CMH and TIRR Memorial Hermann. It takes about two months. We guide you through every step alongside the hospital, and you’ll get a clear checklist when you’re accepted.

How much time does it take?

CMH volunteers give 4 hours per week, in two 2-hour shifts, Monday to Saturday between 9 AM and 7 PM. TIRR Memorial Hermann volunteers give 3 hours per week, in one 3-hour shift, any day: 9 AM to 8 PM for in-patient shifts or 9 AM to 4 PM for Therapy Gym shifts. Committee members give about 1 to 2 hours per week.

What if my class schedule changes after registration?

Shifts are flexible within each hospital’s hours, so you can build your schedule around them. Spring registration opens November 1, and volunteering is a weekly commitment, so pick times you can keep all semester. The application asks you to confirm you can commit to the weekly shifts.

Can I volunteer and be on a committee?

Yes, and many members do both. It isn’t required.

How are members selected?

Volunteer spots are determined each semester based on each hospital’s capacity, and each committee is a small team of about 3 to 4 people. Interviews are on October 20 and decisions go out October 25. If you applied for more than one role, your interview covers each one.

What happens at the interview?

A conversation. We’ll ask about working with patients if you applied to volunteer, and a few questions about each committee you chose.

Do I have to be premed?

No. You do not have to be premed. You just need to be passionate and reliable.

What if I miss the October 15 deadline?

You can apply in the next application cycle for the Fall semester when we open those applications.

Where can I ask questions?

Come to our general body meeting on Monday, October 5 at 6:00 PM in McMurtry PDR, or email ricechampsorg@gmail.com.
