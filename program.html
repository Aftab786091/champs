What CHAMPs is

Rice CHAMPs connects Rice undergraduates with patient-facing volunteer opportunities in the Texas Medical Center. Many students who want extended patient interactions have no clear way to get it, so we're building that path. Our founding cohort will help us grow from two programs into many.

Ways to get involved

Pediatric Volunteer, Children's Memorial Hermann

4 hrs/week

Volunteer with pediatric patients at CMH, in two 2-hour shifts each week.

Choose any two 2-hour blocks, Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM.

Rehabilitation Volunteer, TIRR Memorial Hermann

3 hrs/week

Volunteer with patients in a rehabilitation setting at TIRR Memorial Hermann, in one 3-hour shift each week.

Choose any one 3-hour block, Monday to Sunday: 9 AM to 8 PM for in-patient shifts, or 9 AM to 4 PM for Therapy Gym shifts.

Education Committee

1 to 2 hrs/week

Design educational programming for pediatric patients, in line with CMH policy, that helps normalize kids' hospital stays.

Outreach Committee

1 to 2 hrs/week

Build partnerships with healthcare institutions so CHAMPs can offer more kinds of programs and experiences.

Socials Committee

1 to 2 hrs/week

Grow CHAMPs' presence on campus through advertising, events, and community-building.

Volunteers can also join a committee, and many do. Apply to as many roles as you like.

Volunteers serve at one hospital at a time. We're also exploring additional programs for the future.

One application, one placement

Apply once for everything. If you want to volunteer, choose CMH, choose TIRR Memorial Hermann, or rank both. After interviews, we place each student in a single program based on interviews, your ranking, and each hospital's capacity, so you'll get one offer to accept.

Onboarding

Before volunteering, members complete onboarding. Onboarding is the same for CMH and TIRR Memorial Hermann. We guide you through every step alongside the hospital, and you'll get a clear checklist when you're accepted. It takes about two months, which is why we're selecting our cohort now.

  • Vaccinations
  • HIPAA clearance
  • Volunteer training
  • Orientation

Founding member leadership

CHAMPs is growing from one program into an organization with multiple programs across different institutions. Founding committee members build their committee from the ground up and lead its first initiatives. If you want a hand in shaping what CHAMPs becomes, join us now.

Who should apply

No prior experience is required. We're looking for reliable, curious students who want to contribute.
